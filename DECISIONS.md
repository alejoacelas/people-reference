### Keep admiration, discovery and city planning separate

### Keep private call evidence in the call archive

The [city-map rules](places/AGENTS.md) use private conversations only as leads for public verification. Link into the separate archive rather than copying its personal material here. The collection’s move in `d2d72f9` preserved these boundaries and its independent history.
