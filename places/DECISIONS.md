## Details

### Keep city maps broader than the discovery roster

City pages include both known anchors and unmet candidates; [discover](../discover/README.md) remains limited to unmet people. Keep trip timing in the linked travel record and people research here so updating one does not create conflicting copies.

### Verify private leads before publishing locations

[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) directs research through private call indexes, but those conversations are leads rather than public evidence. Confirm with self-authored or institutional sources; recheck evidence older than 90 days before contact and label intermittent residence. `347abe0` removed reliance on a redundant private place index.

### End with a few concrete next actions

Keep at most five priority contacts per visit, preserve trusted anchors and record named referrals in the discovery inbox. Do not infer friendship from an online mention. This makes maps actionable without presenting a guessed social graph as fact.
