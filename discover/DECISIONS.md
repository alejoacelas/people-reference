## Details

### Keep unconfirmed meeting status in the source inbox

The [roster instructions](AGENTS.md) limit discovery to people Alejo has not met. Supplied lists mix known and unmet people, so preserve names and referral provenance in [sources.md](sources.md) until classified; a source-list entry alone is insufficient.

### Record current location with dated evidence, or Unknown

Prefer self-authored location evidence and retain its date. Do not infer location from an employer or school. Preserve a useful work sample and the nearest human discovery source so the roster supports an informed next action.

### Keep program membership and roles exact

When extending the source inbox, distinguish Foresight Fellows from AI Node grantees and deduplicate people across programs without erasing memberships. Safe AI Germany’s core team is distinct from its advisory board. Preserve these source boundaries instead of quietly broadening a cohort.
